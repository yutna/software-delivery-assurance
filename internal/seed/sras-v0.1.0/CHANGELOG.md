# Changelog

All notable changes to the Software Readiness & Assurance Standard (SRAS) are recorded here.

The format follows semantic versioning:

- **MAJOR** — changes to Gate items, scoring model, or thresholds.
- **MINOR** — new checklist items, modules, templates, or guidance.
- **PATCH** — wording clarifications, typo fixes, or non-normative examples.

---

## [v0.1.0] — 2026-10-22

### Added

- Initial SRAS framework:
  - principles, scope, tiers, roles, and neutrality rules;
  - maturity model L0–L4;
  - criticality model G/H/M/L;
  - Gate rule, Readiness Index, Evidence Coverage, and claim status;
  - Core Checklist covering product, architecture, data, security, reliability, performance, operations, release, quality, documentation, support, compliance, and cost;
  - AI/ML extension with benchmark safety guidance;
  - Integration/API extension;
  - Regulated and high-risk data extension;
  - Cloud/SaaS extension;
  - On-premises extension;
  - evidence standard, freshness rules, redaction, and evidence index;
  - assessment process and sign-off;
  - claim and sales guide;
  - assessment, evidence, risk, model card, assurance summary, claim, release, risk acceptance, and sign-off templates.

### Notes

- This is a draft for internal adoption.
- The standard is neutral by design: no industry, institution, vendor, cloud provider, or jurisdiction is assumed in the Core.
- “Assessed against SRAS” is not a certification.
- Customer, legal, regulatory, and security requirements may be stricter and take precedence.
