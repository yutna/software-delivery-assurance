# ASEAN registers and cross-border transfers

This page compares the six ASEAN register entries. It records what each entry
indexes and how the instrument addresses transfers of personal data across
borders. It is a factual index. It is not legal advice, it does not rank the
instruments, and it does not decide whether a transfer is lawful.

All six entries are `unverified`. No named qualified reviewer has signed off.

## Entries and official sources

Each entry has a generated reference page with its summary, obligations,
mapped requirements, and review status.

### `ASEAN-MCC-001`

The ASEAN Model Contractual Clauses provide a controller-to-processor module
and a controller-to-controller module based on the ASEAN Framework on Personal
Data Protection (2016). They are a voluntary tool and create obligations only
through a contract that incorporates them.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/ASEAN-MCC-001.md)
- [Official source](https://asean.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/ASEAN-Model-Contractual-Clauses-for-Cross-Border-Data-Flows.pdf)

### `ID-PDP-001`

Law No. 27 of 2022 defines categories of personal data, data subject rights,
and the duties of data controllers and data processors. It sets conditions for
transfers outside Indonesia and provides for a supervisory commission.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/ID-PDP-001.md)
- [Official source](https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/832/t/undangundang%20nomor%2027%20tahun%202022)

### `MY-PDPA-001`

The Personal Data Protection Act 2010 regulates personal data in commercial
transactions. It was amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act
2024 (Act A1727), whose provisions commenced in phases.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/MY-PDPA-001.md)
- [Official source](https://lom.agc.gov.my/act-detail.php?act=709&lang=BI)

### `PH-DPA-001`

The Data Privacy Act of 2012 protects personal information in government and
private-sector systems and creates the National Privacy Commission. A
controller remains accountable for personal data transferred to a third party
or another country.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/PH-DPA-001.md)
- [Official source](https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/2012/08/15/republic-act-no-10173/)

### `SG-PDPA-001`

The Personal Data Protection Act 2012 sets baseline obligations for
organisations that collect, use, or disclose personal data in Singapore. A
transfer outside Singapore is permitted only under the conditions the Act sets,
including a comparable standard of protection.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/SG-PDPA-001.md)
- [Official source](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PDPA2012)

### `VN-PDPD-001`

Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP set rules for personal data protection, including
consent, data subject rights, impact assessment dossiers, breach notification,
and cross-border transfer dossiers. It ceased to have effect on 1 January 2026
and was replaced by the Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15 and
Decree No. 356/2025/ND-CP.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/VN-PDPD-001.md)
- [Official source](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?pageid=27160&docid=207759)

## Common observations

These observations are factual and are not a ranking:

- All six entries map to `SRAS-PRV-007` (cross-border transfers).
- Five entries index national instruments. One entry indexes a regional
  voluntary framework.
- Four entries are laws, one is a regulation, and one is a framework.
- One entry, `VN-PDPD-001`, is `repealed`.
- The entries do not share a single transfer mechanism.

## What this page does not decide

This page does not:

- decide whether a transfer of personal data is lawful;
- decide whether an instrument applies to a particular organisation or
  processing activity;
- determine the adequacy of a destination country;
- compare or rank the instruments by strength or coverage;
- state that an organisation, product, or service complies with any instrument;
- create an ASEAN overlay.

An ASEAN overlay does not exist in v0.9.0. A register entry does not add a
requirement and does not replace an assessment of the specific transfer.
