# EU registers and applicability

This page compares the nine EU register entries. It records what each entry
indexes, when the instrument applies, and which SRAS requirements the entry
maps. It is a factual index. It is not legal advice, it does not rank the
instruments, and it does not decide whether an instrument applies to a
particular organisation, product, or service.

All nine entries are `unverified`. No named qualified reviewer has signed off.

## Entries and official sources

Each entry has a generated reference page with its summary, obligations,
mapped requirements, and review status.

### `EU-AIACT-001`

The Artificial Intelligence Act sets harmonised rules for AI systems and
general-purpose AI models. It covers prohibited practices, high-risk AI
systems, transparency obligations, market surveillance, and governance. Its
application is staged, and the later obligations continue into 2027.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/EU-AIACT-001.md)
- [Official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)

### `EU-CRA-001`

The Cyber Resilience Act sets horizontal cybersecurity requirements for
products with digital elements. It covers design and development duties,
vulnerability handling, technical documentation, conformity assessment, market
surveillance, and reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe
incidents.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/EU-CRA-001.md)
- [Official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj)

### `EU-DATA-001`

The Data Act sets harmonised rules on fair access to and use of data. It covers
data generated by connected products, sharing with data recipients, switching
between data processing services, public-sector access, and interoperability.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/EU-DATA-001.md)
- [Official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2854/oj)

### `EU-DORA-001`

The Digital Operational Resilience Act sets requirements for the security of
network and information systems that support financial entities and for their
ICT third-party service providers. It covers ICT risk management, incident
reporting, resilience testing, third-party risk, and information sharing.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/EU-DORA-001.md)
- [Official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj)

### `EU-EAA-001`

The European Accessibility Act sets accessibility requirements for certain
products and services, including websites, mobile applications, e-commerce,
banking services, e-books, transport services, and consumer terminals. It also
sets requirements for accessibility information and conformity assessment.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/EU-EAA-001.md)
- [Official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj)

### `EU-EIDAS-001`

eIDAS establishes a framework for electronic identification and trust services.
It covers electronic signatures, seals, time stamps, registered delivery, and
website authentication, and it sets rules for recognition and supervision.
Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 amended it to establish the European Digital Identity
Framework, including the EU Digital Identity Wallet.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/EU-EIDAS-001.md)
- [Official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj)

### `EU-GDPR-001`

The General Data Protection Regulation sets rules for the processing of
personal data and for the free movement of personal data in the European Union.
It covers processing principles, data subject rights, controller and processor
duties, transfers outside the European Economic Area, and supervisory
authorities.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/EU-GDPR-001.md)
- [Official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj)

### `EU-NIS-001`

The NIS 2 Directive sets measures for a high common level of cybersecurity
across the European Union. It sets risk-management and incident-reporting
obligations for essential and important entities, cooperation rules, and
supervision and enforcement duties.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/EU-NIS-001.md)
- [Official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj)

### `EU-PSD-001`

The Payment Services Directive 2 sets rules for payment services in the
internal market. It covers authorisation and supervision, transparency, rights
and obligations, strong customer authentication, and access to payment accounts
for third-party providers. The Commission proposed a replacement directive
(PSD3) and a Payment Services Regulation in 2023; as of the last verification
date the adopted replacement instruments had not been published in the Official
Journal.

- [Reference page](../reference/registers/EU-PSD-001.md)
- [Official source](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/2366/oj)

## Common observations

These observations are factual and are not a ranking:

- Nine entries: six regulations and three directives, each recorded with
  `instrument_type: regulation`.
- Seven entries apply to all four supported sectors. `EU-DORA-001` applies to
  finance, and `EU-PSD-001` applies to finance and e-commerce.
- Four entries have staged application dates: `EU-AIACT-001`, `EU-CRA-001`,
  `EU-DATA-001`, and `EU-EAA-001`.
- `EU-NIS-001` applies through national transposition measures.
- `EU-EIDAS-001` is recorded as `amended` because Regulation (EU) 2024/1183
  amended it.
- `EU-PSD-001` indexes the directive in force. The replacement PSD3 and the
  Payment Services Regulation were not published as adopted acts on the last
  verification date.
- All nine entries are `unverified` and none is an EU overlay.

## What this page does not decide

This page does not:

- decide whether an instrument applies to a particular organisation, product,
  service, or processing activity;
- determine the adequacy of a country, provider, or control;
- compare or rank the instruments by strength, coverage, or importance;
- determine equivalence between instruments or between SRAS requirements and
  an instrument;
- state that an organisation, product, or service complies with any instrument;
- create an EU overlay.

An EU overlay does not exist in SRAS 1.1.0. A register entry does not add a
requirement and does not replace an assessment of the specific product, service,
or processing activity.
