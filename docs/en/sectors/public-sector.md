# Public sector

The `TH-PUB` overlay adds 12 assurance requirements for government agencies,
state enterprises, and vendors delivering digital public services in Thailand.

## What it covers

- Procurement and contract evidence for each service.
- Records retention on an approved schedule.
- Accessibility of public-facing services.
- Thai-language content reviewed by a native speaker.
- Open data quality, licence, and privacy controls.
- Hosting location and data residency.
- Personnel screening matched to access and sensitivity.
- Annual continuity testing for essential services.
- Auditable and attributable administrative actions.
- Shared change records across agencies and vendors.
- Citizen-facing incident communication with an approved template and owner.
- Privacy impact assessment before public launch.

## Requirements

| ID           | Requirement                                                              |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `TH-PUB-001` | Procurement and contract evidence is retained for each service           |
| `TH-PUB-002` | Records retention follows an approved schedule                           |
| `TH-PUB-003` | Public-facing services meet the agreed accessibility target              |
| `TH-PUB-004` | Thai-language service content is reviewed by a native speaker            |
| `TH-PUB-005` | Open data is published with quality, licence, and privacy controls       |
| `TH-PUB-006` | Hosting location and data residency are documented and approved          |
| `TH-PUB-007` | Personnel screening matches the role's access and sensitivity            |
| `TH-PUB-008` | Essential public service continuity is tested at least annually          |
| `TH-PUB-009` | Administrative actions are auditable and attributable                    |
| `TH-PUB-010` | Cross-agency and vendor changes use a shared change record               |
| `TH-PUB-011` | Citizen-facing incident communication has an approved template and owner |
| `TH-PUB-012` | A privacy impact assessment is approved before public launch             |

The full statements, evidence expectations, and assessment guidance are in the
generated [public sector reference](../reference/overlays/TH-PUB.md).

## What it does not cover

- Procurement law advice or bid protest procedure.
- A legal opinion on data residency.
- A full records-management schedule for every agency.
- A guarantee of accessibility conformance.
- A statement that a service is approved for public launch.

## Related register entries

The public sector overlay references `TH-CCA-001`, `TH-CYBER-001`,
`TH-DGA-001`, `TH-PDPA-001`, and `TH-PROC-001`, plus WCAG 2.2. Read the
[register index](../registers/index.md) for the official sources and the review
limitation.
