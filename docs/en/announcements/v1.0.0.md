## Short announcement

> The Software Delivery Assurance project is preparing the v1.0.0 release of
> its SRAS starter kit: an open, evidence-based way to structure software
> delivery assurance. The kit provides 167 active requirements across a Core,
> five technical modules, and four Thailand sector overlays, plus 27 regulatory
> register entries, seven informative crosswalks, a project-instance CLI,
> deterministic reference pages, and a content guard. It is usable with
> limitations. The Thai translation is machine-assisted and partial, the
> registers are unverified, and no external human review has been completed.
> The kit is not a certification, not legal advice, and not evidence of
> compliance.

## Who it is for

- Thai government digital teams, state enterprises, and vendors delivering
  public services.
- Product owners, engineering leads, security and privacy teams, operations
  teams, assessors, and bid teams.
- Finance, e-commerce, and logistics teams that need the same evidence
  discipline.

## What the release contains

- A copyable project template and a private project-instance workflow.
- The SRAS Core with 70 active requirements.
- Five technical modules with 52 requirements: AI/GenAI, integration and API,
  regulated data, cloud/SaaS, and on-premises.
- Four Thailand sector overlays with 45 deltas: finance, e-commerce, logistics,
  and public sector.
- 27 regulatory register entries: 12 Thailand, 6 ASEAN, and 9 EU.
- Seven informative crosswalks to external standards.
- A TypeScript CLI for `init`, `project validate`, `project refresh`,
  `project resolve`, `render`, and `guard`.
- A bilingual English and Thai documentation site.
- Deterministic reference pages and a content guard.

## What it does not claim

- It is not a certification, accreditation, audit attestation, or regulatory
  approval.
- It is not legal advice or a definitive statement of legal compliance.
- It is not a published standard with external sign-off.
- No external human reviewer has been engaged. The v0.9.0 AI self-review is
  not independent and is not a substitute for external review.
- The Thai translation is `translation_status: partial` with
  `machine_translated: true`. No named human Thai reviewer has confirmed it.
- The 27 register entries are `review_status: unverified`.
- The four legal concerns are desk research notes, not legal opinions.
- The template is generic. It does not encode agency-specific procurement,
  records, or approval rules.
